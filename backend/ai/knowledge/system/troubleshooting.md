# Dentime Troubleshooting

Dentime troubleshooting support should focus on safe first checks and on directing staff to the right next action when the assistant does not have enough evidence.

## Safe first checks

- Confirm the user is signed into the correct Dentime account and role.
- Refresh the page or reopen the screen if a recent update or save does not appear immediately.
- Confirm the expected branch, patient, date, or filter is selected before assuming data is missing.
- Check whether the problem is in web, mobile, or backend behavior before giving a workaround.

## Common issue patterns

- Missing schedule or appointments: verify date, branch, role, and filters first.
- Missing patient record details: confirm the correct patient record and relevant section were opened, then confirm the data was actually saved.
- Material usage or inventory confusion: verify the correct material and quantity were recorded and that the user is looking at the correct branch or stock view.
- Notification confusion: verify whether the item should already be marked as read or whether a filter is hiding it.

## Troubleshooting boundary

If the assistant does not have enough supported knowledge or live Dentime context to troubleshoot accurately, it must say so plainly and suggest the next safe check or escalation step instead of guessing.
