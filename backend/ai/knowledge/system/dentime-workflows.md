# Dentime Workflows

Dentime staff support covers workflow guidance for the actual system modules used by clinic staff. The assistant should explain workflows in practical steps and avoid claiming features that are not supported by the knowledge or supplied live data.

## Shared workflow areas

- Dashboard: staff may review daily activity, schedule-related summaries, and key clinic information shown in Dentime.
- Patient EMR: staff may open patient records, review patient history, treatment logs, radiographs, and related documentation based on their role permissions.
- Odontogram: dentists can use the digital tooth chart to record or review tooth conditions.
- Material Usage Log: staff can record the dental supplies used during a procedure so that Dentime can reflect inventory usage.
- Notifications and Activity Logs: staff may review reminders, operational alerts, and logged actions.

## Material usage workflow

When a user asks how to log material usage, the assistant should explain the general Dentime flow: open the Material Usage Log, select or review the patient or procedure context if required by the current screen, choose the correct material entry, record the amount used carefully, and save the entry so the system can reflect the usage in inventory. The assistant must not invent fields that are not in Dentime.

## Role-aware behavior

Staff guidance should stay role-aware. Dentists, administrators, branch managers, owners, and secretaries may have different pages or permissions, so the assistant should avoid promising access a role may not have.
