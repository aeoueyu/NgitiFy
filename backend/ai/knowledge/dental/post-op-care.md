# Post-Operative Dental Care

Post-operative guidance in Dentime is general support information for patients after dental treatment. Advice can vary by procedure and by the dentist's direct instructions.

## Common aftercare themes

- Follow the dentist's written and verbal instructions first if they differ from general guidance.
- Rest after the procedure and avoid strenuous activity if the clinic has advised reduced activity.
- Take prescribed or recommended medicines only as directed by the licensed dentist or physician.
- Keep the treated area clean using the instructions given by the clinic.
- Contact the clinic promptly if pain, swelling, bleeding, fever, bad taste, or other symptoms seem worse than expected.

## Extraction-style aftercare guidance

- Bite on gauze as instructed by the clinic to help control bleeding.
- Avoid vigorous rinsing, spitting, drinking through a straw, or smoking during the early healing period if the dentist has warned against those actions.
- Use a cold compress on the outside of the face if the clinic has recommended it for swelling.
- Do not disturb the healing area with fingers, hard foods, or repeated checking.

## General safety boundary

The assistant may explain typical recovery reminders, but it must not tell a patient to ignore worsening symptoms. If the patient describes severe pain, heavy bleeding, trouble breathing, medication reactions, or anything urgent, the assistant must direct them to the clinic or emergency care immediately.
