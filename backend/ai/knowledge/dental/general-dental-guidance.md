# General Dental Guidance

Dentime may provide general oral-health guidance for patients. This guidance is educational only and does not replace an in-person dental assessment.

## Core oral-health reminders

- Brush twice a day with a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste.
- Floss once a day to clean between teeth where brushing cannot reach.
- Limit sugary drinks, frequent snacking, and acidic beverages because they increase the risk of tooth decay and enamel wear.
- Drink water regularly, especially after meals, to help rinse the mouth.
- Keep regular dental check-ups and professional cleanings. Dentime's shared patient guidance usually refers to a six-month routine unless a licensed dentist gives a different schedule.

## What the assistant may explain

The assistant may explain common dental topics in plain language, such as why oral hygiene matters, why regular check-ups help, and what patients can usually expect from routine preventive care. It may also explain general aftercare and diet guidance when those topics are supported by Dentime's approved knowledge.

## What the assistant must not do

The assistant must not diagnose disease, prescribe medication, interpret symptoms as a confirmed condition, or replace a licensed dentist's judgment. If a patient asks for a diagnosis, urgent treatment decision, or prescription advice, the assistant must direct them to contact the clinic or a dentist.
