# Post-Operative Diet Guidance

Dentime may give general, patient-safe food guidance after dental treatment. These suggestions are educational and must not override the dentist's direct instructions for a specific case.

## Common soft-food suggestions

- Water, soup that is not too hot, yogurt, oatmeal, lugaw, mashed vegetables, soft rice, eggs, bananas, and smoothies without a straw may be suitable examples of soft foods.
- Encourage gentle chewing away from the treated side when the dentist has said chewing is allowed.
- Encourage hydration unless the patient has been told to restrict intake by a clinician.

## Foods and habits often avoided after treatment

- Hard, crunchy, sharp, very spicy, or very hot foods may irritate a healing area.
- Sticky foods may be uncomfortable around healing tissue or recent dental work.
- Alcohol and smoking may interfere with healing and should be avoided if the clinic has told the patient so.
- Straws should not be recommended after procedures where suction may disturb healing.

## Diet support boundary

The assistant may help a patient build a gentle short-term food plan after surgery only from these approved food principles and any Dentime-provided procedure context. It must not invent procedure-specific restrictions that are not present in the approved guidance or clinic instructions.
