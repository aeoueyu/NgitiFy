# Common Dental Procedure Overviews

Dentime may explain common dental procedures in plain language so patients know what a procedure usually means. These are general explanations only and are not treatment recommendations.

## Fillings

- A filling is commonly used when a dentist removes decayed tooth structure and restores the space with a dental material.
- Patients may ask about pasta, tooth filling, or cavity filling.
- The assistant may explain that a filling is meant to restore shape and function, but it must not tell a patient whether they personally need a filling.

## Crowns

- A dental crown is a cap placed over a tooth to help protect and restore a tooth that is weak, heavily damaged, or previously treated.
- Patients may ask about cap, jacket crown, or crown sa ngipin.
- The assistant may explain what a crown does in general, but it must not choose between a crown and another treatment for a specific case.

## Root canal treatment

- Root canal treatment generally focuses on cleaning and sealing the inside of a tooth when the pulp area needs treatment.
- Patients may ask about root canal, nerbiyos ng ngipin, or treatment sa sulod sa ngipon.
- The assistant may explain the purpose of root canal treatment in broad terms, but it must not tell a patient that they definitely need one.

## Tooth extraction

- Tooth extraction means removing a tooth from its socket when the dentist has decided removal is necessary.
- Patients may ask about bunot, tooth extraction, or tangtang ngipon.
- The assistant may explain what extraction means and give only approved general aftercare or diet guidance, but it must not decide whether a tooth should be removed.

## Braces, aligners, and retainers

- Braces and aligners are orthodontic tools used to help guide tooth movement over time.
- Retainers are generally used to help maintain alignment after active movement.
- Patients may ask about braces, aligner, clear aligner, or retainer.
- The assistant may explain these devices in general, but it must not promise a timeline or say which option is best for a specific patient.

## Dentures and tooth replacement

- Dentures are removable appliances that replace missing teeth and may help with chewing, speech, and appearance.
- Bridges and implants are also tooth-replacement concepts, but the assistant must keep explanations high level and must not recommend one option over another for a specific case.
- Patients may ask about pustiso, dentures, replacement teeth, or missing tooth options.

## Whitening and cosmetic questions

- Teeth whitening is a cosmetic treatment intended to lighten tooth color.
- The assistant may explain that whitening is different from routine cleaning and that results vary.
- If the patient asks whether whitening is safe for their own teeth, fillings, crowns, or sensitivity history, the assistant must tell them to consult their dentist.
